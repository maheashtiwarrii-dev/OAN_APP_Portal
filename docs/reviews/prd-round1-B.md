# PRD Review Round 1 — Reviewer B (Security, Privacy & Governance)

B-1 (High) FR-20/FR-02/FR-05/§4: partner data-access controls. Partner visibility gate (name/headline only if at least one section is Members+Partners); partner match scores use only partner-visible sections; quotas (50 results/query, 200 views and 100 searches/day/user, 1,000 views/month/org); no partner export/print/REST; log every partner search and view, with members able to see which partner orgs viewed them (90 days); bulk-pattern auto-suspension.
B-2 (High) FR-18/FR-20/§4/FR-06: fix partner-contact contradiction. Partners message only (i) in reply to Express Interest or (ii) shortlisted candidates (one initial message until reply); "Allow partner contact" toggle; 20 initial messages/day. Partner initiatives pre-moderated, and recruitment must use FR-05.
B-3 (High) NFR-17/OI-2/§13: compliance detail. DPIA, record of processing with lawful bases, DPAs, cross-border transfer mechanism, breach timelines (72h GDPR / DPB), Grievance Officer with 30-day DSR response, 18+ attestation. New OI-6 (privacy contact / EU representative).
B-4 (High) FR-26/§10: deletion scope (embeddings, notifications, endorsements, feedback anonymised, audit pseudonymised, backups age out ≤60d); deactivated accounts deleted after 12 months; PENDING purged after 90 days, unverified after 7 days; export needs re-auth and a session-bound link; rectification, objection, DPDP nomination.
B-5 (High) FR-19/FR-08/GOV-03: IP and confidentiality controls on import. Rights basis plus attestation; second review for Sales/Product items; screen for "Oracle Confidential" markers; notice-and-takedown within 1 business day; conversation-import notice and removal rights.
B-6 (High) NFR-01/NFR-15: no account linking by email alone; email_verified required; password reset rules; email-change double confirm; re-auth for sensitive actions; MFA for Partner Users; no account enumeration; progressive delay; ORDS OAuth2 and rate limits.
B-7 (High) NFR-05/§4: tamper-evident audit, extended to partner searches/views, exports, privileged reads, consent/visibility changes, authz-denied and AI queries. Moderators see only their own actions.
B-8 (Medium) FR-01/FR-25/FR-11: approval evidence standard (LinkedIn / vouch / document); duplicate and ban-list check; remove employee ID; impersonation report with auto-restrict at 2 or more reports; permanent ban; 14-day endorsement wait for new accounts; reciprocal-cluster report.
B-9 (Medium) FR-01/FR-16/FR-03: define "Chapter" visibility (primary chapter or explicitly shared); max 5 extra chapters; cross-country city chapters need approval; follower controls; blocking removes follows.
B-10 (Medium) FR-24/25/27/07: conflict-of-interest routing; appeal decided by a different person; moderators see only the reported message ±5; private feedback visible to Platform Admin only; Chapter Admin reports aggregate with cells under 5 suppressed; production access policy and masked non-prod data.
B-11 (Medium) FR-AI/§1.4: GenAI provider in the same region with a DPA (no training, ≤30d retention); retrieval through the visibility layer only; sensitive data never embedded; prompt-injection defences; partner AI limits; opt-out deletes embeddings within 24h; AI suggestions labelled, with a human in the loop.
B-12 (Medium) FR-10: non-guessable verification code (16+ chars) separate from the display number; holder can disable public verification; rate limit, noindex, identical response for unknown/disabled codes.
